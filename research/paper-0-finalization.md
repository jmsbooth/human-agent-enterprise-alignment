## Revision intent

Retain Paper 0 as an enterprise-architecture-led conceptual synthesis. Its distinct contribution is the relationship between organizational meaning, actor topology, process state, strategic purpose, governance, and action-specific evidence. Zero Trust PDP/PEP, hierarchical ephemeral delegation, machine-speed changes, and data-centric propagation are introduced as operational implications. Their detailed mechanism design and comparative evaluation belong to Paper 1.

## Specific peer-review challenges

1. Does the additional material actually add useful explanatory depth over Jørgensen & Ma's 2026 bounded-autonomy framework?
2. Can an architect identify source-of-record ownership, context applicability, enforcement responsibility, and outcome evidence for a concrete action?
3. Does the machine-speed argument make a defensible, testable threat assumption without pretending that all organizational contexts change at high velocity?
4. Are ephemeral agents described as one useful execution pattern rather than a settled replacement for persistent and role-specific agents?
5. Is the difference between an authorized action and a useful business outcome maintained throughout?
6. Are technical mechanisms appropriately identified as proposed or illustrative, with implementation claims deferred to Paper 1?
